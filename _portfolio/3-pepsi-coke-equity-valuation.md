*August 2025*

A corporate finance and equity valuation comparing PepsiCo and Coca-Cola.

* Analyzed five years of financial data to assess profitability, liquidity, capital structure, and other ratios.
* Calculated market valuation using WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital), DCF (Discounted Cash Flow), and CAPM (Capital Asset Pricing Model).
* Assessed valuation multiples including P/E (Price to Earnings), EV/EBITDA, and P/S (Price to Sales).

**Focus:** corporate finance, valuation, ratio analysis

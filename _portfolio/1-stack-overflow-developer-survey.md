An interactive intelligence dashboard analyzing the Stack Overflow Developer Survey.

* Developed a multi-page Streamlit platform after comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on 54,000+ global responses, identifying key trends in compensation benchmarks and technology adoption.
* Engineered interactive filtering by country and experience level, enabling granular "User Persona" analysis to understand developer sentiment and tooling frustrations.
* Translated complex datasets into actionable visualizations with seaborn, focusing on industry-specific KPIs such as median salary distribution and remote-work adoption.

**Tools:** Python, Streamlit, pandas, seaborn
